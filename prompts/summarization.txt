## Summarization Objectives

### 1. Topic Clarity

### 2. Contextual Markers
- Temporal references (dates, years, "recently", "now", etc.)

- Key questions being addressed

## Summarization Strategy

Follow this approach:

1. **Opening Context** (1-2 sentences): Establish the primary topic and conversation type
2. **Core Content** (3-5 sentences): Capture the main discussion points and key concepts
3. **Technical Details** (2-3 sentences): Include specific technologies, methods, or terminology
4. **Conclusions/Outcomes** (1-2 sentences): Summarize key insights or resolutions

## Quality Guidelines

- **Preserve Classification Signals**: Maintain words and phrases that indicate domain, complexity, and context
- **Eliminate Redundancy**: Remove repetitive explanations while keeping distinct concepts
- **Maintain Proportionality**: Ensure the summary reflects the relative importance of different topics
- **Keep Technical Precision**: Don't oversimplify technical terms that are classification-relevant
- **Preserve Temporal Context**: Include time-sensitive references that aid in classification
